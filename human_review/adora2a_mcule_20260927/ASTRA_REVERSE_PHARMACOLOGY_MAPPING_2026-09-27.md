## Named pharmacology anchors

- **Istradefylline**: FDA-approved A2A antagonist for Parkinson disease; clinical druggability anchor only.
- **compound 16j**: published potent hA2A antagonist in the 3,5-dimethylpyrazolyl-pyrimidine series; candidate 2 related-series exemplar.
- **ZM241385 / RAD11**: research antagonists anchoring hepatic-fibrosis/MASH mechanistic evidence.
- **CGS21680**: A2A agonist control demonstrating the opposite, context-dependent MASH/HSC direction.

## TCM conclusion

- The strongest currently qualified concrete TCM chain is **虎杖 -> resveratrol -> direct A2A agonist evidence**. Its NASH evidence is preclinical and mixed, and the liver effect has not been proven to be A2A-mediated.
- Curcumin provides a second natural-product chain with A2A activation evidence and separate experimental steatohepatitis/fibrosis evidence, but those mechanisms must not be collapsed into one causal claim.
- None of the five Mcule candidates is established as a constituent of those herbs.
