## If budget is very tight

Buy the **6-item core** first: ZM241385, CGS 21680, Nobiletin, Tangeretin, Salidroside, Dihydrotanshinone I.

## If budget allows a fuller mechanism panel

Add **Dihydromyricetin** and **Baicalin** to reach the 10-item panel.

## Critical interpretation

- **Nobiletin and Tangeretin are especially valuable** because they bridge measured A2A binding with liver/NASH biology, without being identical to the five Mcule hits.
- **ZM241385 + CGS21680** are not discovery candidates; they are required direction/assay controls.
- **Dihydrotanshinone I + Physcion** are discovery bets: source and procurement are clear, but direct A2A activity remains to be tested.
- **Istradefylline** is retained because old-drug repurposing/new-use may be valuable even though the drug itself is not new.
- Vendor pages are evidence of catalog/declared stock only; lot CoA, purity and exact material identity must be frozen before experimental release.
